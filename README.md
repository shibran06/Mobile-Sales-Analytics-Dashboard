#  Mobile Sales Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze mobile sales performance, transactions, customer ratings, payment methods, brands, mobile models, and sales trends.



##  Dashboard Preview

![Mobile Sales Analytics Dashboard](Dashboard%20Preview.png)



##  Project Overview

The **Mobile Sales Analytics Dashboard** transforms mobile sales data into an interactive and visually appealing business intelligence dashboard.

It provides insights into sales performance, product performance, customer ratings, payment methods, geographic sales distribution, and sales trends.

The dashboard allows users to interact with the data using multiple filters and visuals.

##  Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales by City
An interactive map visual showing the geographic distribution of mobile sales across different cities.

### 2. Total Quantity by Month
A line chart showing monthly quantity trends from January to December.

### 3. Customer Ratings
A funnel chart displaying the distribution of customer ratings from 1 to 5.

### 4. Transactions by Payment Method
A donut chart comparing transactions across:

- UPI
- Debit Card
- Credit Card
- Cash

### 5. Brand Performance
A detailed table comparing brands based on:

- Total Sales
- Total Quantity
- Transactions

### 6. Sales by Mobile Model
A horizontal bar chart showing the top-performing mobile models based on total sales.

### 7. Sales by Day
A trend visualization showing sales performance across different days of the week.



##  Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes slicers for:

-  Mobile Model
-  Payment Method
-  Brand
-  Day Name
-  Month

These filters allow users to dynamically explore different aspects of the sales data.

4. Refresh the data.
5. Use the available slicers and visuals to explore the dashboard.
